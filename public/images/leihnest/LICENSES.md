## Decorative concept art

`garden-scene.webp`, `garden-header.webp`, `leaves.webp` and `nest-logo.png` are decorative crops of AI concept art supplied by the project owner in this conversation. They are not authentic photographs of customer groups. No complete mockup screenshot is used as a page, and no control or customer statistic is baked into the interface. The photography licenses above do not apply to this separate artwork.

## Product representation

The six photographs illustrate item types. The application does not claim they depict the group's actual possessions. Authenticated inventory cards identify them as illustrative photos. Manufacturer marks identify the photographed products only, without a partnership or endorsement claim.
